# PDF-informed vs random vs nested training (TEMPORARY)

Full-NLOS users reachable by an EMS, anchors excluded (736 users). Per-user selection of the chart with the highest
label-free trustworthiness. Informed = greedy coverage of the anchors' angles (footprint 0.15 in sin), slot k = k-th
entry of each EMS. Random = K uniform entries per EMS, mean over 10 draws. Nested = ever-finer spread, same entry on
both EMSs. [clusters] = search restricted to the entries within 2 beamwidths of an anchor (greedy: densest first).
Hybrids here use the informed training. SNR cells: median / 10th-percentile gain vs zero phase [dB]; columns = total sweeps.
