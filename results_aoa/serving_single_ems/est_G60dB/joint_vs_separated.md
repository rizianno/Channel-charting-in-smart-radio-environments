# Joint (both EMSs present) vs separated (one EMS deployed): median and RMSE

Full-NLOS users, charts from full-NLOS users, per-user selection of the chart with the highest label-free trustworthiness.
Joint = both EMSs present (training sweep k sets each EMS to its k-th entry; the BS sees both paths; SNR served by both).
Separated = the other EMS removed from training data and served SNR. Same users, anchors and training entries per EMS.
Angle cells: |error| median / RMSE [deg], scored users reachable by that EMS, columns = training sweeps K.
SNR cells: median gain vs zero phase [dB], columns = total sweeps. Joint SNR: users reachable by at least one EMS, served by both.
