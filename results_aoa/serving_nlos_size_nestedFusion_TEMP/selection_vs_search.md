# Per-user chart selection vs search baselines (TEMPORARY run)

Source: `est_G60dB/summary.csv` of this run (same-entry training on the nested spread order,
per-user selection of the chart with the highest label-free trustworthiness).
Users: full NLOS (no direct BS-UE channel), reachable by at least one EMS, anchors excluded.
Cells: median SNR gain vs zero phase [dB] / 10th percentile [dB]; columns = total number of sweeps.

- greedy (sequential search): sweeps the codebook in order and keeps the best entry so far.
- hierarchical search: 4 spread entries, then halving around the best.
- t-SNE selection + LUT: K training sweeps (K = sweeps), chart -> azimuth -> codebook entry.
- + refinement (R): K training sweeps + the LUT entry and its R neighbours (sweeps = K + R + 1).
- + hierarchical (best split): K training sweeps, then hierarchical search warm-started at the LUT
  entry; for each total, the best K is shown (K in brackets), i.e. an optimistic split.
